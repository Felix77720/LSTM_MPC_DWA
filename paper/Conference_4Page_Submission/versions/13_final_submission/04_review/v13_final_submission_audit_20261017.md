## Equations and method description

The main text retains five numbered, used display equations for the state/clearance model, causal LSTM forecast, MPC objective, trust allocation, and DWA safety screen/command envelope. The MPC sequence `u_{1:30}` is defined as the next 30 commands. Variables in the state/clearance equation explicitly identify the predicted UAV position and forecast obstacle centre. The trust equation includes the exponential guide-agreement score `τ_ref = exp(−α_T Δ_p)`; prose defines the mismatch and decay parameter. The four trust weights are tied to the documented 4,320-episode Stage-1 development selection (best five-arm equal-weight mean 0.8042), explicitly not a held-out result. The text distinguishes those selected weights from fixed implementation settings α=1, β=3 and α_T=3 m⁻¹; planned Stage 2 remains incomplete. It explains how the same time-aligned LSTM forecast enters both MPC and DWA, how DWA's guide-dependent sampling budget can also change, and why the frozen contrast estimates the total configured closed-loop effect of enabling MPC's LSTM input rather than an isolated MPC-cost effect. The DWA explanation distinguishes the first 10 forecast states screened in the frozen 1-s benchmark from all 30 states used in the 3-s exploratory evaluation; MPC uses 30 steps in both. It also describes conditional lateral recentering, re-screening and fallback to the originally screened action. Full parameter definitions and implementation details remain in the supplement.

## Evidence boundary and remaining limitations

No simulation was rerun and no outcome records were edited. The frozen benchmark remains 936/1,500 (62.4%) for shared LSTM versus 836/1,500 (55.7%) for DWA-only LSTM, +6.7 percentage points (95% scene-cluster CI 4.2–9.2). In the separate exploratory evaluation, shared LSTM reached 221/300 (73.7%), its DWA-only LSTM contrast was inconclusive, and standalone MPC-CV had the highest rate (276/300; 92.0%) with 15 swept-contact runs. These results support a protocol-specific forecast-sharing gain, not overall superiority over independent DWA/MPC or global optimality. Figure 4 is one matched example and does not replace those statistics. Evidence is simulation-only, prescribed obstacle paths are used, and the planned independent confirmation and real-flight validation remain absent.

Ten retained BibTeX records were field-checked against publisher, proceedings, author-institution or supplied publisher-formatted sources; five are cited in the compiled paper. No metadata was guessed or added to the manuscript without verification. Full source-by-source findings, including fields that remain unverified for uncited records, remain in `04_review/reference_verification_v13.md`. The earlier MPC–DWA paper is not emphasized or cited in the main-text narrative; the comparator is described in the supplement as a project-local reconstruction.

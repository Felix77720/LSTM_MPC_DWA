# Version 13 AI-assisted review archive

This folder contains 48 internal review-process records moved from the active Version 13 `04_review` folder so they can be found together. They include simulated referee-style reports, review syntheses, self-reviews, revision checks, response notes, the archived Round 10 review packet, and the earlier Revision 09 final-review set.

These are AI-assisted author-side review artifacts. They are not reports from an external conference or journal peer-review process and should not be presented as such. The authoritative final audit, experimental protocols, reference checks, figure QA, and statistical evidence remain in the [Version 13 review folder](../../Conference_4Page_Submission/versions/13_final_submission/04_review/).

## Reading order

1. `review_report.md` and `revision_response.md` — initial assessment and response.
2. `review_round2_*` through `review_round7_*` — early review cycles and syntheses.
3. `review_round10_packet.md`, then `round10_*` through `round16_*` — later review packet, checks, and response cycles.
4. `historical_final_referee_*_revision09.md` and `historical_final_review_synthesis_revision09.md` — preserved Revision 09 final-review set; `final_referee_*.md` and `final_review_synthesis.md` are the later author-side review set.
5. `main_paper_self_review.md`, `table_redesign_self_review.md`, and `post_revision_assessment*.md` — focused self-review records.

The original files were moved without editing their contents. Earlier version snapshots retain their own historical review files.
